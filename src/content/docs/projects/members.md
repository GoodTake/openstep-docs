---
title: Invite members
description: Add people to the workspace by wallet address on the Members page.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Click **Members** in the left rail to open `/projects/members`. The heading is **Manage who can access this workspace**.

If you can manage members, the top-right action is **Add wallet**. The dialog title is **Add workspace member**. Enter a **Wallet address** (`0x...`) and click **Add member**. The copy explains that the added wallet can view every project in this workspace.

There is no separate invite-link page. Add the other person's wallet address.
