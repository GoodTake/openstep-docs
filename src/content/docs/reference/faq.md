**Why do I sign twice?**  
First your wallet connects, then it signs a login request. The second step is not a transfer: the page says **It's free — no transaction, no gas.** See [Sign in](/start/sign-in/).

**What is the difference between an agent and an App?**  
An agent holds your conversation, its plan and its builds. It builds one or more Apps, and each App has its own preview, code, data and live address. See [What is OpenStep](/start/what-is/).

**How do I use a ready-made agent?**  
Pick one in the **Marketplace** and click **Use this agent →**. See [Pick an agent from the Marketplace](/agent/pick-agent/) and [Create your first agent](/start/create-agent/).

**Why didn't Brainstorm change my app?**  
That is by design. **Brainstorm** asks questions and drafts a plan, and it builds only after you approve the plan. To change the app right away, send your message in **Build** mode. See [Build and Brainstorm](/build/modes/) and [Approve the plan](/agent/approve-plan/).

**What is the difference between Preview and Publish?**  
**Preview** is your draft, the app as it is right now, before anyone else can open it. **Publish** puts every App of the agent live together, at addresses other people can open. See [Preview your site](/build/preview/) and [Go live](/build/go-live/).

**How do I let other people try my app?**  
Publish the agent, then send them the address. You choose who can open it, anyone with the link or only learners, in **Console**, **Settings**, **Access**. See [Publish and share](/agent/publish-and-share/).

**Can I take payment in another currency?**  
No. In-app payment is SSU only. See [What is SSU](/account/what-is-ssu/).

**Where did my SSU go?**  
Click **Balance** in the left rail. **Activity** lists every charge for building, for the platform features your apps use, and for running an app live. See [Check your balance](/account/balance/).

**Can I work on an agent from my own computer?**  
Yes, with the OpenStep CLI. See [What the CLI does](/cli/what-it-does/).
