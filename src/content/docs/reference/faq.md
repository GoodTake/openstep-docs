**Why do I sign twice?**  
First connect the wallet, then sign a login request. The second step is not a transfer. The page says **no transaction, no gas**.

**Why didn't Brainstorm change my app?**  
That is by design. Brainstorm only discusses. Click **Start building** to write code.

**What is the difference between Preview and Publish?**  
Preview is the draft you are editing. Publish pushes a version to a URL other people can open.

**Can I take payment in another currency?**  
No. In-app payment is SSU only.
