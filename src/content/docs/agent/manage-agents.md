Your agents are listed in **My agents**, and each agent has its own settings in the Console. The workspace that holds them has settings of its own.

## My agents

Click **My agents** in the left rail. The page says "Everything you own or collaborate on, newest activity first." and has **+ New agent** at the top right.

1. Use the tabs to filter the list: **All**, **Needs your answer**, **Building**, **Ready to publish**, **Live** and **Draft**. To find one by name, type in **Search agents…**.
2. Read the cards. Each one shows a status badge (**Building**, **Live**, **Draft** or **Ready to publish**), the agent's name, a line about what it is doing now (such as **Planning**, **Working** or **Unpublished changes**), the agent it is based on, and when it was last active.
3. Click a card to open the agent's work page.

   ![The My agents page with filter tabs and a grid of agent cards, including Three Day Strength marked Building.](/images/agent/manage-agents-1.webp)

Agents other people shared with you appear here too, with your access level. Learning experiences you joined stay in **Campus Earth**.

## Change one agent

Open the agent, click **Console** in the header, then **Settings**.

- Rename. Under **Display**, change **Agent name** and click **Save changes**. Here you can also pick an **Avatar** colour and a **Card image**, with **Upload image** or **Generate**. **Language** and **Tone** change how the agent writes and speaks, and apply the next time it builds. Owners and editors can rename.
- **Duplicate agent**. Under **Ownership and workspace**, click **Duplicate agent**. You get a new agent with a copy of every App's code, to change without touching the original. The data, values and secrets stay with the original. The box beside the button suggests `<agent name> (copy)`; type another name there if you want to choose it.
- **Move to another workspace**. Open the menu that says **Choose a workspace**, pick one, and click **Move**, then confirm with **Move agent**. Its Apps and their data go with it, and members of that workspace can view it. Its collaborators lose their roles on it, and invite links not yet used stop working.
- **Transfer ownership**. Open the menu that says **Choose a collaborator**, pick the person, and click **Transfer ownership**, then confirm with **Transfer**. You stay on the agent as an editor, and only the new owner can publish it, change who can open it or delete it. The agent needs a collaborator before you can transfer it. See [Work with others](/agent/work-with-others/).
- **Delete agent**. At the bottom, under **Danger zone**, click **Delete agent**. The dialog **Delete agent permanently** says that this removes the agent, its files, its conversation, every App and their data, and that it cannot be undone. Type the agent's name to confirm, then click **Delete agent**.

Only the owner can duplicate, move, transfer or delete an agent. **Access** and the collaborators list are on the same page, and are also owner-only. See [Publish and share](/agent/publish-and-share/).

## Workspace settings

Click **Settings** in the left rail, under **WORKSPACE**. The page says "Manage the workspace that holds your agents." You can also reach it from the workspace menu at the top of the rail, under **Workspace settings**.

- **GENERAL** has the workspace **Name** and its **Slug**. In a team workspace the owner can change the name and click **Save**. The name of a personal workspace is fixed.
- **Usage & balance** shows your **Available balance** in SSU and the number of **Your agents** in this workspace.
- **CLI ACCESS** lists the devices you have authorized to act as you through the openstep command line, each with **Revoke**. See [Install and sign in](/cli/install/).
- **TRANSFER OWNERSHIP** gives a team workspace to another active member: choose a **New owner**, click **Transfer ownership** and confirm with **Transfer**.
- **DANGER ZONE** is only in team workspaces. The owner can **Delete workspace**, which permanently deletes it and every agent in it, after typing its name to confirm. Other members see **Leave workspace** instead.

**Members** in the left rail lists the people in the workspace. See [Work with others](/agent/work-with-others/).

## Good to know

- Each agent keeps its own settings for collaborators, access and publishing. The workspace page does not change them.
- You cannot delete an agent while it is still being set up. Wait for setup to finish.
- Deleting a workspace deletes every agent in it, so move out any agent you want to keep first.

That is the whole path for a ready-made agent. To build a website instead, start with [Start a website](/build/start-website/).
