When the last task is done, the app is ready to try in **Preview** on the work page. This is your development copy. You can use it and test it as much as you like before anyone else sees it.

1. Look at the right side of the work page. The building card has been replaced by the App. If the Console is still open, close it with **✕**. Above the App you will find the **Preview** and **Data** tabs, a page menu that starts on **Home**, a menu to switch between desktop and phone size, and **Open ↗**, which opens the preview in a new tab.
2. Look around. Use the page menu to move between the App's pages. The Health & fitness App holds the strength plan for three 45-minute gym sessions a week, with guidance for each exercise. The plan and the exercise pages are open to read without signing in, and each page carries a short health note.
3. Log a workout. Your training log is personal, so the App asks you to sign in with your wallet before it saves anything. Then start a day from the plan, save each set as you do it, and finish the workout. It appears in your history.
4. Log your weight. The App keeps a private daily weight with its trend, lets you set a goal, and has a place for body measurements. It uses kg and cm, the units you chose when you answered the questions. Only you can see your entries.

   ![The Three Day Strength app open in Preview on the work page.](/images/agent/use-your-app-1.webp)

5. Open the **Data** tab to see what the App saved. It shows "View and manage the data stored in your app." with one card per table and its row count. Open a table to see its rows. Log another workout in the preview and you can watch the rows appear.
6. Ask for changes. Keep chatting in the message box on the left. For example, ask for another training day, different wording, or a different look.

## Good to know

- What you do in **Preview** is development data. It is kept apart from the data of the live app after you publish, so you can test freely. See [Site data](/build/data/).
- **Open ↗** is handy for checking the app at its real size, or on your phone.
- A small change, such as new wording, a number, a colour or an image, is made at once. A bigger change in **Brainstorm** goes through the questions and plan again, and you approve the plan before anything is built. See [Build and Brainstorm](/build/modes/).
- You can read the App's code in the Console under **Code**, but you change the app by asking in the chat. See [Browse the code](/build/code/).
- Nothing is public yet. The app goes live when you publish it.
