After you sign in you land on **Home**. The left rail is on every page, so you can get anywhere from it.

![Home with the left rail on the left: New agent, Home, Marketplace, My agents, Campus Earth, Members, Settings, recent agents, and your balance at the bottom.](/images/start/find-your-way-1.webp)

## The left rail

From top to bottom:

- Workspace switcher (**Personal** to start with): the workspace you are working in. Your agents belong to a workspace.
- **+ New agent**: opens **New agent**, where you start an agent. See [Create your first agent](/start/create-agent/).
- **Home**: your starting page (see below).
- **Marketplace**: the ready-made agents you can start from. See [Pick an agent from the Marketplace](/agent/pick-agent/).
- **My agents**: everything you own or collaborate on, newest activity first. Filter it by status, such as **Building** or **Live**. See [Manage your agents](/agent/manage-agents/).
- **Campus Earth**: the learning experiences you joined, such as a course you were invited to. Your own agents stay in **My agents**.
- **Members** (under **WORKSPACE**): the people who collaborate in this workspace, and **Invite member**. See [Work with others](/agent/work-with-others/).
- **Settings** (under **WORKSPACE**): the workspace name, your usage and balance, the devices authorized for the OpenStep CLI, and transfer of ownership.
- **RECENT AGENTS**: your latest agents, one click from the left rail.
- **Balance**, at the bottom: your SSU balance. Click it to open the Balance page. See [Check your balance](/account/balance/).
- Your wallet, below **Balance**: opens the account menu.

## Home

**Home** shows:

- **Welcome back**, with **Total agents**, **Building** and **Needs attention**, your **Platform balance** in SSU with a **+ Top up** button, and **Wallet assets**.
- **What would you like to build?**, the same box as on **New agent**. Describe what you want, pick **Build** or **Brainstorm**, and start. See [Create your first agent](/start/create-agent/).
- **Recently edited**, a card for each of your latest agents with a **Live** or **Draft** badge. **View all agents →** opens **My agents**.

## The account menu

Click your wallet at the bottom of the left rail. The menu includes:

- **Balance**: the same page as **Balance** in the left rail.
- **Purchase SSU**: buy SSU. See [Get SSU](/start/get-ssu/).
- **+ Redeem a code**: use a course code. See [Redeem a code](/account/redeem/).
- **Disconnect**: sign out.

## Good to know

- On an agent's work page the left rail collapses to icons so the conversation and the app have more room.
- Everything you create lives in **My agents**. Courses you join live in **Campus Earth**.
