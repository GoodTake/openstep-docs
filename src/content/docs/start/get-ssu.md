SSU is the balance OpenStep charges for building, for the platform features your apps use, and for running an app live. There are three ways to get it: buy it on **Purchase SSU**, top it up from SSU you already hold in your wallet, or redeem a course code.

## Buy SSU

1. Open the account menu at the bottom of the left rail and choose **Purchase SSU**. The page shows **Base mainnet** at the top right.
2. Enter the amount under **SSU AMOUNT**, or click a quick pick: **100**, **500**, **1,000** or **5,000**. You can buy 100 to 10,000 whole SSU at a time.
3. Under **PAYMENT**, choose **USDC** (**On Base**) or **Card** (**Stripe**).
4. Check the summary: **You pay** and **You receive**. The page shows the price before you pay. **Destination** is your **Builder Smart Account**, where the SSU arrives.
5. Click the button for the way you chose:
   - **Recharge with USDC**: your wallet asks you to approve the exact USDC amount (**Approve exact USDC**), then to **Confirm purchase**. The page walks through **Switching to Base**, **Confirming on Base** and **Sending SSU**, then says **The destination balance is ready.**
   - **Continue to Stripe**: Stripe Checkout opens and you pay by card. Keep the page open while the payment completes. If you cancel, the page says **Stripe Checkout was cancelled. No charge was made.**

![The Purchase SSU page with 500 SSU entered and USDC chosen, before any payment.](/images/start/get-ssu-1.webp)

When the purchase is done your balance updates. Check it on the [Balance](/account/balance/) page.

## Top up from SSU in your wallet

If your wallet already holds SSU, you can move it to your platform balance:

1. Click **Balance** at the bottom of the left rail.
2. Under **Top up**, enter an amount under **TOP UP AMOUNT**, or click **Max**. The page shows your **Wallet balance**.
3. Click **Top up** and approve the transfer in your wallet.

If you already sent the SSU yourself, paste the transaction hash under **Already sent? Paste transaction hash** and click **Submit**.

## Redeem a course code

If a course gave you a code, redeeming it joins you to the course and gives you the SSU that comes with your seat. Choose **+ Redeem a code** in the account menu, or **Have a course code? Redeem it →** on the sign-in page. See [Redeem a code](/account/redeem/).

## Good to know

- **Buying with USDC** needs USDC in your wallet on Base and a little ETH on Base for gas. If either is short, the page says **Your wallet does not have enough USDC.** or **Your wallet needs more ETH on Base for gas.**
- **Card details stay with Stripe.** You enter them on the Stripe Checkout page, not on OpenStep.
- While a build or an app call is running, some of your SSU is **Held for running work**. Each charge shows up under **Activity** on the [Balance](/account/balance/) page.
- You can refund SSU you have not spent to your wallet from the [Balance](/account/balance/) page.
