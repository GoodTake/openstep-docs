Open [builder.openstep.academy](https://builder.openstep.academy). There is no username or password: your wallet is your account. If you are not signed in, you land on the **Sign in** page.

1. Pick a wallet. **Base** (Base Account) and **Coinbase Wallet** are listed under **BASE APP**; **OKX Wallet**, **WalletConnect**, **Rainbow** and **MetaMask** are under **OTHER WALLETS**.
2. Approve the connection in your wallet. The page says **Approve the connection in your wallet.**
3. Approve the signature request in your wallet. The page says **It's free — no transaction, no gas.** If the request does not show up, click **Send it again**.

After your signature is verified you land on **Home**. If you were sent to the sign-in page from another page, you return to that page instead.

![The Sign in page: wallets listed under BASE APP and OTHER WALLETS, and a link to redeem a course code.](/images/start/sign-in-1.webp)

## Good to know

- **OpenStep runs on Base.** If your wallet is on another network, the page says your wallet is on the wrong network and **STEPS runs on Base. Switch, and we'll pick up where you left off.** Click **Switch to Base** and sign-in carries on.
- Signing in is free. It sends no transaction and spends no gas, and OpenStep never sees your private keys.
- To use a different wallet, click **Use a different wallet** while you sign in.
- If you are already signed in and open the sign-in page, you go straight to **Home**.
- If you have a course code, use **Have a course code? Redeem it →** below the wallet list. See [Redeem a code](/account/redeem/).
- To sign out, open the account menu at the bottom of the left rail and choose **Disconnect**.
