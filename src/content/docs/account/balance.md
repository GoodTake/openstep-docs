Open the account menu → **Credits** to go to `/funds`. The page explains **Move SSU between your wallet and your Smart Account.**

Two directions:

- **Deposit**: from the wallet into the Smart Account
- **Withdraw**: from the Smart Account back to the wallet (the page shows a fee; the default display is 0.5 SSU)

The wallet must be on **Base**, and the connected address must be this account's owner. The coin icon in the left rail shows the same SSU balance. It is not a link — use **Credits**.
