Click **Balance** at the bottom of the left rail to open the Balance page. The **+ Top up** button on **Home** opens the same page.

![The Balance page with the Available, Held for running work and Balance tiles, Top up and Refund on the left, and Activity on the right.](/images/account/balance-1.webp)

## The three tiles

- **Available**: the SSU you can spend now.
- **Held for running work**: SSU set aside while a build or an app call is running.
- **Balance**: the SSU your account holds on the platform.

## Top up

**Top up** moves SSU that your wallet already holds to your platform balance. The page shows your **Wallet balance**. The steps are in [Get SSU](/start/get-ssu/), together with the other ways to add SSU: buying it, and redeeming a course code.

## Refund

**Refund** sends SSU you have not spent back to your connected wallet.

1. Enter an amount under **REFUND AMOUNT**, or click **Max**. The page shows how much is **Available**.
2. Click **Request refund**.

Your refunds are listed under the button. Until you ask for one it says **No refunds yet.**

## Activity

**Activity** is a list of every change to your balance, newest first, each with its SSU amount:

- Charges for building and for the platform features your apps use.
- **Live runtime**, with the agent's name and the day, for running an app live.
- **Top up**, **Course grant** and **Refund** rows.

Click **Load more** to see older rows.

## Good to know

- **Top up** and **Request refund** use your connected wallet. It must be the wallet you signed in with. If it is on another network, the page asks you to switch to Base mainnet and offers **Switch network**.
- Charges come out of your balance by themselves, while you build and while an app runs. To see what spends SSU, read [What is SSU](/account/what-is-ssu/).
