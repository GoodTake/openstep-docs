---

SSU is the token OpenStep uses. Two main things consume it:

1. **Building itself** spends SSU (chat, generation, and platform capabilities).
2. **If your published app takes payment**, in-app payment is SSU only — no other method.

The left rail shows the current balance. **Credits** in the account menu opens the funds page.
