SSU is the token OpenStep uses. You keep a balance of it on the platform, and three things spend it:

1. Building. Each turn the builder works on an agent is charged in SSU.
2. Platform features your apps use, such as AI calls, email and contract operations.
3. Running an app live. After you publish an agent, keeping its Apps online is charged to your balance too.

If your published app takes payment from its own visitors, in-app payment is SSU only. OpenStep has no other payment method.

## Where you see your SSU

**Balance** at the bottom of the left rail shows your SSU. Click it to open the Balance page: what you can spend, what is held for work in progress, and every charge. See [Check your balance](/account/balance/).

## How to get SSU

- Buy it on **Purchase SSU**, from the account menu. See [Get SSU](/start/get-ssu/).
- **Top up** from SSU your wallet already holds, on the Balance page. See [Check your balance](/account/balance/).
- Redeem a course code, if a course gave you one: choose **+ Redeem a code** in the account menu. See [Redeem a code](/account/redeem/).
