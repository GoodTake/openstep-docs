---
title: Redeem a code
description: Redeem a course code at /redeem to unlock the course workspace and the SSU that comes with it.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Open `/redeem`, or choose **+ Redeem a code** in the account menu at the bottom of the left rail, or follow **Have a course code? Redeem it →** on the sign-in page. The heading is **Redeem your course code**.

1. Enter a code like `STEPS-ABCD-EFGH-IJKL` in **Course code**. When the page recognizes the code, a card under **Unlocks** shows the course and the SSU it adds.
2. If you are not signed in, the page says **Code checked. Connect a wallet below to claim it.** Under **Step 2 · Connect your wallet**, pick a wallet. If you are signed in, click **Redeem code**.
3. On success the page says **Workspace created**, with the course name and the SSU it gives you. Click **Open your workspace**.

The SSU can arrive a little after the workspace is ready. Click **Refresh status** to check. Your [Balance](/account/balance/) page shows it once it is in.

You do not need an agent first. If the code is invalid or revoked, already used by another wallet, or temporarily unavailable, the page shows the reason. If you try too many times it asks you to wait a minute. Other ways to get SSU are in [Get SSU](/start/get-ssu/).
